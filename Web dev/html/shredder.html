THE PROBLEM
You printed it.
But it never left.
Every day, students and professionals walk into a print shop, hand over a file containing their assignments, resumes, or personal documents — and walk out with only the printout. The file stays behind. Nobody tells you. Nobody asks.
🎓
The Student
Neha submits her thesis draft at a campus print shop. She collects her copies and leaves. Her file — 60 pages of research — remains on the system.
🏪
The Print Shop
The shop's system stores every job in a local folder. No deletion policy. No encryption. Files accumulate for months, sometimes years.
⚠️
The Risk
Anyone with system access — a staff member, a hacker, or a curious employee — can open those files. Personal data. Academic work. Private records.

"73% of print shops in a 2024 survey had no formal file-deletion policy."

THE INSIGHT
Temporary files.
Permanent storage.
The design of print shop software was built for convenience, not privacy. Files are sent to a queue, printed, and then — simply forgotten. Nobody built a delete button. Nobody thought they needed one. Until now.

THE SOLUTION
Shredder. A privacy-first print system built for the real world.
Shredder replaces the traditional print shop workflow with one that guarantees your file is gone the moment your job is done. No logs. No folders. No trace.
🔒
Privacy by Default
Every print job auto-deletes on completion. No manual step required from shop or user.
📲
Token-Based Access
Users receive a one-time QR token. No account. No login. No trace left behind.
⏱
Countdown Timer
Files are accessible for a limited window only — then wiped automatically by the system.
🛡
Zero Storage Policy
Shredder never writes files to persistent storage. Guaranteed at the architecture level.
For Users:
Print with confidence. Your documents belong to you — and only you.
For Shops:
Offer a premium, privacy-compliant service. Zero liability. Maximum trust.

HOW IT WORKS
Three steps. One promise.
Shredder fits into the existing print shop experience — with no hardware changes required.
01
Upload & Configure
  • Open Shredder app or web portal
  • Choose print settings (copies, size, B&W)
  • Set a self-destruct timer: 5, 15, or 30 min
›
02
Get Your Token
  • Receive a unique QR code on your phone
  • QR is valid only for your session
  • Walk to the nearest verified Shredder shop
›
03
Print. Then Shred.
  • Scan QR at the shop's Shredder terminal
  • File decrypts, prints, then deletes instantly
  • Confirmation: ✓ File Shredded
